The itch

Work runs a weekly team quiz. When it was my turn, I decided to build the quiz with AI instead of writing it by hand. The AI I have at work is Copilot Cowork, so that's what I used.

How it came together

Under an hour. It's a single web page: a lobby where everyone joins, then eighteen famous Australians, from Don Bradman to Margot Robbie. Each one comes with six clues. Guess early and you score more; hang on for more clues and the points drop. The page keeps score for the whole team.

What broke

The clues came together fast. Most of my changes went into the scoreboard: making the score tracking easier to follow while the whole team is playing.

What happened next

It got played, and it became the base for later quizzes. Change the topic, have the questions generated, and it's ready.

One thing I'd now always do: generate a separate answer sheet for the host.

What I'd tell myself

Build as you go. It's quick to recover from a mistake and quick to make changes.
