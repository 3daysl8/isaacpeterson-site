⚠ Scope limitation — exempt workers, coal miners & volunteers excluded

These reforms apply to workers covered under the NSW Workers Compensation Acts. They do not apply to:

  • Exempt workers (police officers, paramedics, firefighters)
  • Coal miners
  • Volunteers

Practitioners managing claims in these sectors must refer to the relevant exempt worker provisions.
